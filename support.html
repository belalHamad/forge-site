Getting started

What are Build, Destroy and Tasks?

Build is for habits you want to grow, like reading or drinking water. Destroy is for habits you want to break, like smoking or too much coffee. Tasks are simple things to get done. Tap a habit to mark it done; for a habit with a count, each tap adds one step.

What are packs?

Packs are ready-made sets of habits for a particular part of life. The Islamic pack is the first, with prayer times, dhikr and other worship habits. More packs are on the way.

Apple Health

Why isn't my Health habit updating?

Open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps → Forge, and check that Forge is allowed to read that type of data. If a habit still looks out of date, open Forge: it checks Health again each time you do.

What does Forge write to Health?

Only what you really logged, such as a glass of water or the time of a habit timer you ran. Marking a habit Complete never writes to Health, and Forge never writes steps.

Prayer times

Why do my prayer times look wrong?

Check that Forge can use your location: iOS Settings → Apps → Forge → Location → While Using the App. Then in Forge, open Profile → Settings → Prayer Times and choose the calculation method used where you live.

Reminders

Why don't my reminders arrive?

In Forge, open Profile → Settings and make sure Notifications is on. Also check iOS Settings → Notifications → Forge, and that a Focus mode isn't silencing it.

Can I pause everything while I'm away?

Yes. Profile → Settings → Vacation Mode pauses your streaks and points for the dates you choose.

Purchases

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Profile → Upgrade to Forge Premium → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Forge.

Your data

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. Forge has no servers, no ads and no tracking. Read the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Profile → Settings → Delete All Data erases everything Forge stores on your iPhone, after asking you to confirm. Entries Forge added to the Health app stay there until you delete them in Health.
